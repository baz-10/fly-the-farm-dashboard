# Spray Command Platform Identity and Organisation Assisted Support Design

Date: 4 August 2026  
Status: Product Owner approved design

## Objective

Separate Spray Command's platform identity from every customer organisation, replace Fly The Farm branding in platform-owned surfaces, and provide time-limited Organisation Assisted Support without granting permanent tenant access to platform administrators.

This work preserves organisation-owned operational evidence, organisation branding, report history, tenant isolation and the existing Production Beta mission lifecycle.

## Governing identity planes

### Platform identity

The platform identity owns platform configuration, infrastructure, subscriptions, billing, Chemical Intelligence, Operational Knowledge, AI and global administration.

Platform identities do not require an organisation membership or licensed organisation seat. A Platform Super Administrator has no automatic access to customer operational data.

For Production Beta, `ben@trollope.com.au` is reconciled as a Platform Super Administrator. The account must not automatically belong to Fly The Farm and must not automatically receive a Fly The Farm seat, location assignment or tenant role.

### Organisation identity

Organisation identities own tenant-scoped customers, properties, fields, jobs, Missions, aircraft, equipment, personnel, chemicals, documents and reports.

For Production Beta, `ben@flythefarm.com.au` remains a Fly The Farm Organisation Administrator. Its permissions, membership, seat and operating-location assignments remain limited to Fly The Farm.

Authentication identity and Personnel identity remain separate. Operational history and approvals continue referencing authoritative Personnel records.

## Slice 1: Platform identity and rebrand

### Brand ownership

Spray Command branding appears on:

- login, registration, password recovery and authentication lifecycle screens;
- global navigation and shared platform chrome;
- Platform Administration;
- platform-owned notifications and empty/error states;
- platform intelligence and global configuration surfaces.

Organisation branding appears only in the active organisation workspace and organisation-owned reports. A workspace must visibly identify the active organisation without presenting that organisation as the platform owner.

Reports remain organisation branded and retain the mandatory footer `Generated by Spray Command`. Existing immutable report artefacts are not rewritten.

### Visual system

The platform identity is minimal, professional and industrial. It communicates precision, control, command, navigation, waypoints and operational excellence. It must not use drones, propellers, leaves or spray droplets as brand symbols.

The visual system uses a restrained waypoint/navigation mark, disciplined alignment, technical neutral surfaces, high-contrast command typography and a dark command-green accent. Decorative imagery is excluded from administration and authentication.

The platform logo and name are repository-controlled assets. Organisation logos continue to use internal file IDs, immutable versions and checksums.

### Platform administration boundary

Platform Administration is a separate application area and permission plane. It does not derive access from an organisation administrator role. Fly The Farm branding, membership labels and customer-operational navigation must not appear in this area.

The generic Administrator role must not implicitly receive platform permissions. Platform permissions are assigned through platform roles only.

## Slice 2: Organisation Assisted Support

### Support request

An authorised Organisation Administrator creates a support request containing:

- organisation;
- reason;
- requested access mode: read only or read/write;
- scope type: whole organisation, specific Mission, specific Job or specific module;
- scope reference where required;
- requested duration;
- approving organisation user and Personnel context where available.

The Production Beta default duration is two hours. The server calculates and enforces the expiry. Future policy may configure permitted durations without changing the workflow.

### Support session

A support session is a dedicated delegated-access record, not an organisation membership, role, seat or location assignment. It records:

- immutable session ID;
- support request and approval;
- platform user;
- organisation;
- access mode;
- scope type and reference;
- reason;
- start time;
- expiry time;
- end or revocation time;
- session state;
- activity summary.

Only appropriately permissioned platform support users may start an approved session. Starting a session does not create tenant membership.

### Enforcement

Every supported request resolves normal server-authoritative identity first, then resolves an active delegated support session. Access is allowed only when all of the following match:

- platform user;
- organisation;
- access mode;
- requested resource or module scope;
- session state;
- current time before expiry.

Read-only sessions reject every mutation. Resource-scoped sessions reject sibling resources. Module-scoped sessions reject other modules. Whole-organisation support still remains limited to the explicitly approved access mode.

PostgreSQL policies and trusted functions reinforce the same boundary. Provider SDKs and browser state never grant support access.

Sessions expire automatically and may be revoked immediately by an authorised Organisation Administrator. Expired or revoked sessions fail closed.

### Audit, activity and outbox

The platform records append-only evidence for:

- request creation;
- organisation approval or rejection;
- session start;
- records viewed;
- mutations attempted and completed;
- session expiry;
- organisation revocation;
- session end.

Every event records the actor, organisation, support session, scope, resource identity, timestamp and outcome. Sensitive payloads are minimised and no secret credentials are stored in activity records.

Support lifecycle changes and material activity create audit events and transactional outbox events atomically with the authoritative transaction.

### Notifications

Organisation administrators receive notifications when access is granted, started, ended, expired or revoked. Notification delivery is durable and failure-visible. Notification failure does not expand or prolong support access.

### Break Glass architecture

Emergency Break Glass is a separate future workflow with a separate permission, explicit reason, additional audit and automatic organisation notification. It is disabled by default and is never a fallback for ordinary support.

This release may reserve provider-neutral interfaces, permission catalogue entries and state-model boundaries for Break Glass, but it must not enable emergency operational access.

## Data model impacts

Repository-controlled migrations introduce platform roles and permissions independently from organisation roles, plus dedicated support request, approval, session and activity records.

Identity reconciliation must be explicit and idempotent. It must not create duplicate internal users or Personnel records. It must preserve historical Missions, approvals, audit events and report evidence.

Existing organisation memberships are not silently reassigned by email-matching application logic. The production reconciliation migration or controlled administration command must validate the exact existing identities before removing any incorrect Fly The Farm membership from the platform account.

## API boundaries

Platform Administration and support-session APIs remain versioned under the trusted server-side API. Domain and application services remain portable and independent of Vercel and Supabase SDKs.

The browser receives only the minimum platform or organisation context required for the active surface. Support-session credentials are server-resolved and must not be represented as reusable browser-managed permissions.

Unsupported scopes, expired sessions, stale versions and cross-tenant references fail visibly without creating partial records.

## Error handling

- Missing organisation approval: deny support-session start.
- Expired or revoked session: deny access and identify the session state.
- Scope mismatch: deny without revealing the existence of out-of-scope records.
- Read-only mutation: deny before domain mutation.
- Stale request or session version: return optimistic-concurrency conflict.
- Notification failure: retain the authoritative access event and expose retry state.
- Identity reconciliation ambiguity: stop without modifying memberships.

## Testing and acceptance

Implementation is test-first.

Slice 1 is accepted when:

1. Login, authentication lifecycle, navigation and Platform Administration use Spray Command identity.
2. No Fly The Farm branding appears in platform-owned administration.
3. The Fly The Farm workspace retains its organisation identity.
4. Reports retain organisation branding and `Generated by Spray Command`.
5. Platform roles do not imply organisation memberships, seats or locations.
6. The two approved Production Beta accounts resolve to their correct identity planes.

Slice 2 is accepted when:

1. A platform administrator has no automatic Fly The Farm operational access.
2. An Organisation Administrator can request and approve a scoped, time-limited support session.
3. Read-only, read/write, whole-organisation, Mission, Job and module scopes are enforced server-side and by PostgreSQL.
4. Automatic expiry and immediate revocation fail closed.
5. Access does not create a membership, seat, role or location assignment.
6. Views and mutations create attributable activity, audit and outbox evidence.
7. Organisation administrators receive lifecycle notifications.
8. Refresh, re-login and a second authorised session retain authoritative state without local or legacy persistence.
9. Tenant and operating-location isolation remain enforced outside the delegated scope.
10. Break Glass remains disabled.

## Migration and rollback

Schema changes are additive before identity reconciliation. The platform rebrand is independently reversible at the presentation layer. Support grants fail closed if the support feature is disabled.

Production identity reconciliation runs only after backups, local verification and confirmation of the linked Spray Command Production Beta Supabase project. If reconciliation cannot uniquely identify both approved accounts, deployment stops for Product Owner review.

Rollback disables new support-session creation and terminates active sessions before reverting application code. Authoritative audit, outbox, notification and historical session records are retained.

## Out of scope

- permanent platform access to tenant operations;
- automatic tenant membership for platform users;
- ordinary-user identity linking;
- enabled Emergency Break Glass access;
- white-label removal of Spray Command report attribution;
- rewriting historical reports or Mission evidence;
- redesigning organisation-owned operational workflows.
